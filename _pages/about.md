I am a post-doctoral researcher in Economics at the <a href="https://www.ehesp.fr/" target="_blank"> French School of Public Health (EHESP)</a>. I completed my PhD at the University of Rennes and hold a Master’s degree from the Toulouse School of Economics (TSE) as well as an engineering diploma from the French Civil Aviation School (ENAC). My research mainly focuses on  health and labor economics.
Before starting my PhD, and after obtaining my Master’s degree, I worked for seven years as an analyst and revenue manager in the air transport industry.
