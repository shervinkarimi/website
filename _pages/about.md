I am a post-doctoral researcher in Economics at the <a href="https://www.ehesp.fr/" target="_blank">French School of Public Health (EHESP)</a>. I completed my PhD at the <a href="https://crem.univ-rennes.fr/en" target="_blank">University of Rennes (CREM)</a> and hold a Master’s degree from the <a href="https://www.tse-fr.eu/?lang=en" target="_blank">Toulouse School of Economics (TSE)</a> as well as an engineering diploma from the <a href="https://www.enac.fr/en" target="_blank">French School of Civil Aviation (ENAC)</a>. Before starting my PhD, and after obtaining my Master’s degrees, I worked for seven years as an analyst and revenue manager in the air transport industry.

I am an applied economist working on the organization of healthcare markets (hospitals and community care) and firm ownership structure, with an interest in public policy evaluations.<br>

**Main field:** Health Economics<br>
**Secondary fields:** Labor Economics, Organizational Economics<br>
