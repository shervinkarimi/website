I am a post-doctoral researcher in Economics at the <a href="https://www.ehesp.fr/" target="_blank">French School of Public Health (EHESP)</a>. I completed my PhD at the <a href="https://crem.univ-rennes.fr/en" target="_blank">University of Rennes (CREM)</a> and hold a Master’s degree from the <a href="https://www.tse-fr.eu/?lang=en" target="_blank">Toulouse School of Economics (TSE)</a> as well as an engineering diploma from the <a href="https://www.enac.fr/en" target="_blank">French School of Civil Aviation (ENAC)</a>. My research mainly focuses on  health and labor economics.

Before starting my PhD, and after obtaining my Master’s degree, I worked for seven years as an analyst and revenue manager in the air transport industry.

I am an applied economist working on healthcare organization, firm ownership structure, and more broadly on the evaluation of public policies.
**Main field:** Health Econimics
**Secondary field:** Labour Economcics, Organizational Economics
